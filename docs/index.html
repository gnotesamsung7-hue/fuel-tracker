FillUp+ is an app for logging vehicle fuel fill-ups, trips, and expenses.

Data this app stores

All data you enter (vehicles, fill-ups, odometer readings, costs, notes, maintenance reminders) is stored locally on your device only. Nothing is uploaded to a server, and no account is required to use the app.

Location

The "nearby fuel stations" feature, if you use it, asks for your device's location to find fuel stations close to you. That location is sent only to OpenStreetMap's public Overpass API (overpass-api.de) to run the search -- it is not stored by this app, and this app does not track your location in the background. Declining the location permission just means that one feature won't work; everything else in the app is unaffected.

Camera

The receipt scanner opens your device's own camera app to take a photo of a receipt. This app never accesses the camera hardware directly. The photo is processed entirely on your device (via Google's ML Kit on-device text recognition) to read the receipt's total, volume, and price -- the photo and its text are never uploaded anywhere.

Internet access

This app only makes a network request for the nearby-stations search described above. Every other feature -- logging, the dashboard, exports, maintenance reminders -- works fully offline.
